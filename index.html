How the matrix is built

Rows follow the AI stack, because a gap in the data layer creates different harm than a gap in an agent’s permissions. Columns are Microsoft’s six Responsible AI principles. Each control is tagged with the NIST AI RMF function it serves: Govern, Map, Measure, or Manage.

How the tiers work

  • Tiers are cumulative. Medium inherits every Low control, and High inherits both.
  • Tier is set at intake from impact, data sensitivity, and autonomy, and is reviewed whenever an agent gains new tools or data.
  • Controls marked “Process” are owned by people, not platforms: review boards, owners, audits.

Frameworks referenced

Microsoft Responsible AI principles, NIST AI Risk Management Framework, ISO/IEC 42001, and the EU AI Act’s risk-based approach.
