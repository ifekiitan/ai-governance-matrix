# Governing agentic AI across the Microsoft stack

A portfolio project showing how Microsoft’s six Responsible AI principles can be turned into concrete, risk-tiered controls across the platforms that enforce them: Fabric, Purview, Foundry, Copilot Studio, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Entra, Defender, and Sentinel.

## What’s here

**[Interactive control matrix](https://ifekiitan.github.io/ai-governance-matrix/)**
72 controls mapped across four layers of the AI stack (data, model, agent, security) and six principles (fairness, reliability and safety, privacy and security, inclusiveness, transparency, accountability). Each control is tagged with its enforcing platform and its NIST AI RMF function. Switch between Low, Medium, and High risk to see how requirements build up.

**[Case study: governing a high-risk HR agent](case-study-hr-agent.md)**
The matrix applied to a realistic scenario: an HR policy agent built in Copilot Studio on Fabric data. It covers each phase from intake to retirement, who owns what, which teams collaborate and when, a 16-week timeline, the issues the controls caught, and post-launch metrics.

## Frameworks referenced

Microsoft Responsible AI principles, NIST AI Risk Management Framework, ISO/IEC 42001, and the EU AI Act’s risk-based approach.
